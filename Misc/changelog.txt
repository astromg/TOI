<h4>Whats New?</h4>

--- 27.10.2026 --- <br>
Windows are now panels of the observation window: drag, tab or float them; the layout is remembered per screen
setup, ⚙ menu brings any panel up and resets the layout. Panel titles show the active telescope and its colour.
<br>
New Aux tabs: Telescope (OCA manual page) and Tasks (for observers, empty for now).
<br>
Status lamps: NATS, dome follower, FITS downloader, OFP, flat and weather overwatch (alive RPC, needs updated oca_overwatch). Rotator: mechanical and EQ angle,
SET moves the mechanical angle (limits ±160°). Gain / read mode defaults from the TIC config. Python 3.14 allowed.
<br>
Observers of the night: a window asks once a day who observes (list in observers.yaml, previous night pre-checked),
shown next to PING; "Only this TOI" sets them for this session without sharing. Panels scroll only on small screens.
<br>
Seeing: the observation window shows the site seeing at zenith from weather_overwatch (median of science frames of
all telescopes, last 30 min), coloured by the limits of all telescopes: the first limit passed yellow, the last red,
the ones between orange (seeing 1.5 / 2.0 / 2.5).
Zero point: the same for the photometric zero point (median of zero_monitor of all telescopes, limits -0.1 / -0.2,
as oca_monitor; the Conditions plot lines moved to these values).
<br>
Calibration: above the flats, age of the last master zero and master dark (flat_overwatch, from the pipeline journal)
and of the last object in any filter.
<br>
Plan: test=True set in the OB edit / add window did not reach the FITS header (the planrunner only reads 1/0);
Test is now saved as 1 / 0.
<br><br>
--- 26.10.2026 --- <br>
TPG: "Make SKYFLATS" adds evening and morning sky flats to the plan.
<br>
New Aux window with tabs: Telescope (OCA manual page), Optical path, Calibration, Focus, Guider, Plan Runner,
Conditions. Tabs come up by themselves (M3 move, flats, focus).
<br>
Focus: Recalculate no longer moves the focuser - use SET. Exposure time and filter added.
<br>
Main window: NATS lamp, wind and direction coloured by per-telescope limits, UT coloured by Sun altitude.
<br><br>
--- 25.10.2026 --- <br>
Optical path: a change that moves something big (the telescope and the dome to the flat screen, the mirror covers by
day) waits for a person - CONFIRM in the status line on every TOI, whoever asked (you or a planrunner); click CONFIRM,
the button says what will move, click again within 5 s to move, or Reject. WAITING: light still to come (the sky at
dusk), SET PATH prepares the path now. "Confirm by hand..." is a table: a button per position of each part no sensor
reads. The optics journal shows in the log. Requires ocabox 2.12, ocabox-common 1.13, datamodels 0.7, optics service 1.7.
<br><br>
--- 24.10.2026 --- <br>
Night monitor option added to tpg window.
<br><br>
--- 21.10.2026 --- <br>
Optical path: values come straight from the subscriptions (ocabox 2.11); NO DATA says why (TIC or the optics service silent). Dome flats with the shutter closed work:
point the telescope at the flat screen, confirm the flat screen lamps by hand (the button appears whenever the chosen
function needs it), SET PATH opens the covers. Requires ocabox 2.11, ocabox-common 1.12, optics service 1.6.
<br><br>
--- 20.10.2026 --- <br>
Sky window, Optical path tab: the telescope drawn as a tree - the trunk in the middle, the camera and BESO branches
on both sides of M3 - with every label placed where it covers no line. Requires ocabox-common 1.10.
<br>
Optical path panel redone in TOI's look: the path ribbon (with what the instrument sees), FUNCTION with SET PATH,
and one status line - READY, SETTABLE, BLOCKED, NOT POSSIBLE, MOVING, FAILED - saying what to do in plain words.
Confirm by hand appears only when a part on the path has no reading. Requires ocabox-common 1.9.
<br><br>
--- 19.10.2026 --- <br>
Optical path (jk15). Instrument tabs show what the detector sees, the path to it from the light source (sky, lamp)
and the functions it can be switched to (SET PATH); unknown parts are named with their cause and can be declared by
the operator. The sky window has an "Optical path" tab with the whole telescope. Requires the optics service.
